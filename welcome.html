BIOREACTOR REFERENCE

Biochemical Equations

Key metabolic reactions and bioprocess equations used in bioreactor systems.

Energy conversion

Photosynthesis

6CO2 + 6H2O + light energy → C6H12O6 + 6O2

Energy conversion

Aerobic Respiration

C6H12O6 + 6O2 → 6CO2 + 6H2O + energy (ATP)

Fermentation

Alcoholic Fermentation

C6H12O6 → 2C2H5OH + 2CO2 + energy

Fermentation

Lactic Acid Fermentation

C6H12O6 → 2C3H6O3 + energy

Anaerobic digestion

Methanogenesis

CO2 + 4H2 → CH4 + 2H2O

Nitrogen cycle

Nitrification — Step 1

NH4+ + 1.5O2 → NO2− + 2H+ + H2O

Nitrogen cycle

Nitrification — Step 2

NO2− + 0.5O2 → NO3−

Nitrogen cycle

Denitrification

2NO3− + 10e− + 12H+ → N2 + 6H2O

Biomass growth

Cell Growth Rate

dX/dt = μX

X = biomass concentration; μ = specific growth rate

Biomass growth

Monod Growth Model

μ = μmaxS / (Ks + S)

S = substrate concentration; Ks = half-saturation constant

Bioprocess yield

Substrate Consumption

dS/dt = −(1 / YX/S)(dX/dt)

YX/S = biomass yield from substrate

Oxygen transfer

Oxygen Transfer Rate

OTR = kLa(C* − CL)

kLa = volumetric mass-transfer coefficient
